# Support

Causure is an early alpha research and engineering project. It is not currently
offered with a service-level agreement, production support contract, or guaranteed response
time.

## Public questions and defects

Use the public repository's structured issue forms for reproducible bugs and bounded feature
or research proposals. Include the exact version, environment, and synthetic reproduction.
Do not post customer content, credentials, personal data, production traces, or signed
artifact URLs.

## Security reports

Do not open a public support issue for a suspected vulnerability. Follow
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) and use the repository's private vulnerability-reporting path.

## Company adoption

The repository documents local and single-host reference boundaries, not a hosted service.
Companies evaluating it remain responsible for authentication, authorization authorities,
secret management, immutable retention, deployment controls, provider accounting, disaster
recovery, and qualification on their exact infrastructure. A public proposal can discuss a
non-confidential integration gap; private operational data must stay in the company's own
systems.
